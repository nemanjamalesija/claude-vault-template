## Rules that hold from day one

- **Never commit or push in project repos on your own initiative.** Committing
  there happens only on my explicit ask. The vault is the exception — the
  sweep rule in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` stands.

  (where a file goes, what gets shared with a team) and I haven't said yes, it
  stays a proposal — never written into vault pages as settled fact.
